Common Questions

Before You Call

Are you really open 24 hours?

Yes. Nights, weekends, and holidays. If your system goes down at 2am in July, you can call right then at (915) 800-1188.

My AC is running but blowing warm air. What's wrong?

Usually one of a few things — low refrigerant from a leak, a failed capacitor, a dirty condenser coil, or a frozen evaporator. Some of it is a quick fix, some isn't. Give us a call and we'll walk through what you're seeing before anyone comes out.

Do you repair appliances too, or just HVAC?

Both. Alongside heating and cooling we handle refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, and ranges — so a broken fridge and a broken AC are the same phone call.

Do you work on commercial refrigeration?

Yes — walk-in coolers, freezers, reach-ins, and ice machines. Restaurant and retail equipment failures are a big part of why we stay open overnight.

What areas do you cover?

The greater El Paso area, including Horizon City, Socorro, San Elizario, Canutillo, Fort Bliss, and Sunland Park. If you're not sure whether you're in range, just call and ask.

Should I repair my old system or replace it?

Depends on the age of the unit, what failed, and what the repair costs relative to a new system. We'll tell you honestly which way the math points instead of pushing a replacement by default.
